# Write Lecture Notes: full audit

Apply these standards within the scope selected in [SKILL.md](../SKILL.md). This reference does not initiate a separate review pass or expand the task.

## Audit before delivery

Recheck the scope map against the final organization: each subsection should advance its parent section's learning target, each concept should have one primary treatment, and transitions should lead into the next topic without duplicating it. Confirm that moved material has the prerequisites it needs at its destination and that no necessary content or references were lost.

Check that section preludes motivate the subject through meaningful phenomena or questions, without learning objectives or computational conventions. Verify that technical remarks sit in the relevant subsections or subsubsections with their prerequisites in place, and that notation and computational conventions are followed throughout the actual material. Audit each subsection's exercise bank for connected breadth: motivated phenomena, technical development, computational experiments, and simple real-data exploration and visualization where relevant. Apply the detailed criteria in [exercises](exercises.md); a minimum exercise count alone is insufficient.

Revisit the concept-to-figure plan first. For every high-priority visual need, identify the completed figure and explain which feature conveys the intended insight; verify that shared figures genuinely cover all assigned concepts. Check for major visually teachable ideas left unsupported, rather than counting figures alone. Confirm that each developed figure has a defined setup, a visible mechanism or comparison, integrated interpretation, and a truthful caption. Verify numerical claims and relevant simulation uncertainty, and inspect all new or modified figures at their final rendered size. Report any incomplete high-priority visual coverage. In the delivery summary, briefly identify the concepts whose visual explanations were added or substantially improved, rather than reporting only formatting and compilation checks.

Read through the entire changed module in order. Check the definition-to-example sequence and result-to-proof-to-calculation sequence, headings, notation, references, figure explanations, exercise placement, and transitions. For each subsection, explicitly verify that all four required boxes—Core Reading, Guiding Questions, Review Questions, and Further Reading—occur exactly once each, are visibly labeled and substantive, and appear in the prescribed order. Confirm that supporting subsubsections do not duplicate these boxes and that the parent boxes cover their material. Check that newly introduced datasets have nearby ggplot2 exploratory displays with documented preparation and substantive interpretation. Audit error notation for conventional symbols and distinguish errors from residuals. Check both reading boxes for verified chapter/section references, and check that the exposition addresses the guiding questions. A substantively revised subsection is incomplete if any required box is missing or duplicated; do not report it as finished. Check every example and exercise for concise motivation, essential rather than tedious calculations, and a clear connection between its requested or displayed output and its teaching purpose. For examples, verify that the body delivers a distinct insight at the intended level; retain simple illustrations of abstract definitions, but consolidate duplicate lessons and remove arbitrary arithmetic filler. Check that each figure panel contributes to that insight and that prerequisite material is recalled rather than unnecessarily redeveloped. Recheck dependent exercises after changing any example or figure. Then map its key concepts to nearby exercises, confirm at least three substantive problems, and check that data-based tasks are executable as written. Check that algorithm schematics clarify meaningful structure and do not merely duplicate pseudocode. Check that pseudocode is language-neutral and complete enough to implement in R or Python, that actual code is labeled and executable, and that all essential illustrations and conclusions appear in the notes. A reader should be able to understand the lecture without opening the companion. Verify any changed companion chunks in a fresh session. For LaTeX output, compile when feasible and inspect the rendered pages for overfull equations, broken references, misplaced figures, and unreadable displays. Report any unresolved mathematical or source uncertainty explicitly.
