## Final audit and delivery

Use this index in the existing verification pass; detailed guidance lives in the linked references. Before delivering, check:

- [Design](design.md): questions have clear purposes and outputs, adequate guidance, and reasonable workload; research extensions are bounded and supported where included.
- [Data and computation](data-computation.md): included datasets and displays serve the question; access, provenance, generation details, numerical summaries, and benchmarks are verified.
- Models, notation, conditional independence, constants, scales, and moment conditions are consistent.
- Tasks are distinct from hints; simulation instructions are executable and comparisons meaningful.
- Cross-references, question labels, point totals, and logistics agree with the final structure.
- The source compiles and the rendered PDF is readable, with no overflow or missing material.

Report the main changes and provide the final file locations concisely. Do not repeat the full assignment in chat or add extra exercises as unsolicited follow-ups.
