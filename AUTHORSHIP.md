# Authorship and provenance

Author and maintainer: **Sheng**, GitHub [sj156](https://github.com/sj156).

This repository shares a portable edition of the author's personal
`write-lecture-notes` skill. It records teaching preferences and workflows for
exposition, exercises, figures, computation, notation, and editorial review.

Codex assisted in preparing this distribution, including generalizing local
course paths and writing repository documentation. This statement describes
provenance; it does not assert exclusive rights over general pedagogical methods,
mathematical ideas, or machine-generated material.

The public skill edition omits personal storage locations and does not assume
that a particular style is installed. The supplied personal LaTeX package is
included separately at `latex/myMath-notes.sty`, with its content unchanged and
its filename normalized to its declared package name. Original personal
installations are maintained separately. No lecture manuscripts or private
course documents are included.

Please cite the author, repository, and the exact release or commit used, when
use has been authorized. Citation and Git history support attribution but do not
by themselves establish legal ownership or grant permission.
